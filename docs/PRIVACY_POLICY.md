# Privacy Policy — Keep Yapping & Everyone Escapes

_Last updated: July 2026_

**The short version: this app collects nothing and sends nothing.**

## Data collection

Keep Yapping & Everyone Escapes does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any data.
The app:

- has **no accounts** and no sign-in,
- makes **no network requests** — it works entirely offline,
- contains **no ads, analytics, or trackers**, and
- requests **no device permissions** (no camera, microphone, location, or contacts).

## Data stored on your device

To support session review by the supervising educator, the app stores the following
**only on the device itself**:

- mission history (mission codes, puzzle outcomes, attempt counts, durations),
- optional tally marks entered by the supervising adult,
- optional short team labels typed at mission setup (we recommend initials or nicknames,
  never full names), and
- display preferences (text size, contrast, motion settings).

This information never leaves the device unless the user explicitly exports it (for
example, sharing a CSV file via the system share sheet). Any session can be deleted at any
time in the Logbook, and deleting the app removes everything.

## Children's privacy

The app is designed for use in schools under adult supervision. Because it collects and
transmits nothing, there is no personal information for us to receive, retain, or disclose,
consistent with COPPA and FERPA expectations.
