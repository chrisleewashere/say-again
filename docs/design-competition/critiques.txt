===== judge:slp — winner [2], ranking [2,0,1] =====
OVERALL: Design 2 (pocket-litter alibi-breaking) wins on the two axes that matter most to a skeptical SLP: it is the only design where the target language function is structurally unavoidable (facts invisible to the Agent, warrants too arbitrary to guess, icons unlabeled so describing is mandatory, the WHY committed as the cited breaking item) and its exchanges are the densest and most varied (naming, two-hop chains, negation flips, fits-vs-breaks near-misses, relevance rulings, forced synthesis recap). Its honest weakness is cognitive load — Mastermind's 24 icons and the left/right cuff pair need fixing before art, and icon nameability needs the pilot it proposes. Design 0 is a solid second: cleanest per-clue commit cadence and the lovely trace-prediction beat, but its commit carries no justification component, so the because-chain the module exists for is purely procedural. Design 1 places third despite the best commit mechanic and the best accessibility: its Rookie tier functionally breaks the two-way gap (commonsense rules over Agent-visible conditions) and its on-screen cover captions undercut the expressive-primary claim — as drafted it is the design a pair can most easily beat with generic talk.
GRAFTS: From Design 0: the Handler's prediction question — before opening the Evidence Index, ask 'what do muddy boots tell you about a person?' and let the Agent hypothesize the proof before the printed row confirms it. This is the one genuine inference-GENERATION beat in any design and Design 2's procedure step 2 currently jumps straight to the lookup; add it as a printed step and as hint 2.
  - From Design 0: clue-linkage chips on committed stamps — when a clear commits, mark the card with the number of the case fact it broke ('broke fact 2'), so the pair can retrace the whole chain at debrief and the SLP can tally which fact each because-sentence connected. Directly strengthens Design 2's forced final recap.
  - From Design 1: the exhaustive table-level solvability test — beyond 1000-seed property tests, enumerate every case x fact-bijection x pool draw at the table level to prove the generator can never dead-end for any seed, turning Design 2's pool-size arithmetic (thin at Mastermind: a 2-icon neutral pool) from an argument into a checked invariant.
  - From Design 1: an SLP-toggleable icon-caption accessibility option — one-word labels under evidence icons, off by default. Design 1's captions are wrong as a default (they gut expressive load) but right as an accommodation for students with severe word-finding deficits, directly answering Design 2's biggest flagged risk without weakening the module for everyone else.
  - From Design 1: an UNLESS-style exception row as a future Mastermind-plus lever — one Evidence Index row with a printed exception ('a dripping umbrella proves rain — unless the file says the courier crossed the canal ford'), reproducing Design 1's clinically excellent near-miss ruling-out language inside Design 2's frame if pairs plateau on the base tables.
