## Description

> One of you sees the puzzles. One of you holds the manual. Nobody has the whole picture — so keep yapping!
>
> Keep Yapping & Everyone Escapes is a two-player cooperative game built around real communication. The Field Agent holds the device and describes what they see. The Handler holds the printed Field Manual (free PDF, two reading levels) and works out the rules. Cracking the safe, bypassing the laser grid, and spotting the secret contact all take one skill above all: talking clearly and asking good questions.
>
> Designed by-and-for school speech-language pathologists — and genuinely fun for any pair who likes escape rooms.
>
> FOR STUDENTS AND FAMILIES
> • Seven original spy-mission puzzles, each with three difficulty levels
> • Endless variety — every mission code builds a different set of puzzles
> • No timer unless you want one, and nothing ever explodes: a tripped alarm just means "talk it over and try again"
> • Swap roles every mission — describing and directing are both skills
>
> FOR EDUCATORS AND SLPs
> • Pick puzzles by communication goal: following multi-step directions, describing and directing, asking clarifying questions and repairing breakdowns, vocabulary and categorization
> • Discreet tally counter (correct / prompted / incorrect) while students play
> • Automatic session logs with CSV export — data never leaves the device
> • Mission codes make any session exactly repeatable
> • Manual comes in standard and easy-read editions
>
> PRIVACY
> No accounts. No ads. No tracking. No internet needed. Nothing is collected — everything stays on your device.
